## How to insert a working tag

1. Open the LinkedIn post composer.
2. Type `@` followed by the first few letters.
3. **Select the entry from the dropdown** — the name turns into a styled link.
4. Plain text such as `@INSTANT` creates no tag and notifies nobody.

A tag was not inserted automatically: that needs an authenticated LinkedIn
session, and publishing anything to your account without your approval was out
of scope.
